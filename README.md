MirrorMe — as the name suggests — is built on one powerful idea:
To help people see themselves through the eyes of the world.
We all know apps like NGL or similar anonymous feedback tools.
But they offer just one basic feature: a link for anonymous messages, with very limited engagement and no deeper value.
MirrorMe takes that same core concept, but expands it into a complete self-discovery ecosystem.
________________________________________
⭐ What MirrorMe Actually Does
MirrorMe allows users to:
•	Share their MirrorMe link across social platforms
•	Receive anonymous insights about their personality, strengths, habits, and blind spots
•	Explore a beautiful, modern interface inspired by apps like Instagram, Snapchat, and Twitter
•	Interact with viral tools, templates, challenges, and sharable stories
•	Earn MirrorCoins and rewards for consistent engagement
•	And most importantly—
Interact with their personal AI coach that analyzes the feedback they receive.
This AI coach helps users:
•	Understand the meaning behind the feedback
•	Identify patterns
•	Track their emotional and personal growth
•	And guide them with actionable self-improvement steps
So MirrorMe is not just a fun tool — it becomes a personal development companion powered by feedback + AI.

1) Game ideas (designed for MirrorMe)
Each is short, viral-friendly, and ties into MirrorCoins / rewards and shareability.
A. Trait Prediction Quiz
•	Friend sees your MirrorID → predicts 3 traits for you → you reveal what others predicted vs. actual.
•	Shareable “Predictions vs Reality” card.
•	Monetize: premium reveal themes, animated reveals.
B. Guess How People See You
•	A mini-game where 5 anonymous responses are shown as hints and player guesses the top trait.
•	Rewards for correct guesses (MirrorCoins).
•	Viral: users challenge friends to beat their score.
C. Daily Reflection Roulette
•	Spin a wheel to get a daily micro-challenge (e.g., “Show appreciation to someone today”, “Post a confidence story”).
•	Completes tasks to earn MirrorCoins and unlock persona badges.
D. Mirror Match (Vibe Match)
•	Match with other users based on persona embeddings.
•	Mini-game shows two persona cards and asks: “Would you vibe with them?” → builds match score.
•	Option to opt-in for discoverability.
E. Persona Builder Challenges
•	Weekly themed challenges (e.g., “Kindness Week”, “Confidence Sprint”) with step-by-step micro-tasks judged by AI & peers.
•	Leaderboards for colleges / groups.
F. MirrorBot Mini-Quests
•	Short interactive story-like chats where the AI coach gives scenarios and the user chooses responses → builds a “growth path” and earns insights.
•	Great retention: daily episodes.
G. Feedback Bingo
•	5×5 card filled with traits; each anonymous piece of feedback marks a square.
•	Completing lines earns rewards; share completed Bingo cards on socials.
H. Aura Remix (Creative Filter Game)
•	Users upload a selfie, then the AI generates 3 “aura” personas (Calm / Magnetic / Playful).
•	Users pick their favorite / remix it → share as reel. (Opt-in, privacy-safe.)
I. Story Poll Templates & Quiz Stickers
•	Pre-built Instagram/WhatsApp story templates that ask followers to vote on traits (integrates with MirrorID).
•	Highly shareable (growth engine).
